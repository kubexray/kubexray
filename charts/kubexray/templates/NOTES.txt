{{- if eq .Values.auth.mode "token" }}

Open it:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ include "kubexray.fullname" . }} 8080:{{ .Values.service.port }}
  TOKEN=$(kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get secret {{ include "kubexray.tokenSecret" . }} -o jsonpath='{.data.token}' | base64 -d)
  open "http://localhost:8080/?token=$TOKEN"

Scripts can send the token as a header: curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" http://localhost:8080/api/history
